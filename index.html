Good to know.

The questions people ask before they book.

Do you really answer at 3am?

Yes. Technicians are on call 24 hours a day, every day, for heating and cooling emergencies. Call or text (646) 315-2271 whenever it happens.

Will I know the price before you start?

Always. We diagnose the fault, walk you through the options and quote the work. You decide before anything begins.

Are estimates free?

Estimates for new systems and replacements are free, with no obligation to go ahead.

Should I repair or replace?

It depends on the system's age, condition and what the repair costs against a replacement. We give you an honest recommendation either way — if repairing makes more sense, we'll say so.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, through Synchrony and FTL Finance. Most people can apply in a few minutes. Approval is subject to credit.

What are your office hours?

Monday to Friday, 9am to 9pm. Weekends by appointment. Emergency service runs 24 hours, all year.
